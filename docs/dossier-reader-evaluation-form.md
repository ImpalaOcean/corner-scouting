# Dossier reader evaluation form (blank protocol)

Status: prepared, not administered. Internal readers: Mohamed and Asaad, independently. This evaluates report clarity, traceability and limitations; it is not football ground truth or a substitute for held-out model scores. Use one recording template per reader and dossier/team view. Do not consult the other reader's answers until both reviews are saved.

Before reading record reader, date, dossier path/SHA-256, team, fixture, cutoff and form version. Read only the prematch report and supporting historical evidence; target outcomes are unnecessary. Record time separately if measured, without claiming comparability to generation benchmarks.

For each checklist item answer yes/no/unsure/not-applicable and cite the report section or supporting ID; explain every no/unsure/NA. Leave unanswered items blank, never default yes.

| ID | Question and evidence criterion |
| --- | --- |
| R1 | Are observed match/corner counts and denominators clear next to the delivery and shot rates? |
| R2 | Are historical-frequency intervals shown and distinguished from uncertainty of model estimates? |
| R3 | Are training eligibility and small-sample/grid-edge limitations visible? |
| R4 | Is the cutoff explicit, with all historical examples strictly earlier and the whole target date excluded? |
| R5 | Can a delivery statistic and a shot statistic each be traced to the listed historical corner IDs and source records? |
| R6 | Are model expectations clearly separated from observed frequencies and provider xG? |
| R7 | Are unknown categories, unavailable 360 and rare-goal limitations clear, without implying downloads-disabled means provider absence? |
| R8 | Does the report avoid causal effectiveness, unsupported tactical labels and certainty about an upcoming result? |

Comprehension prompts (free text, no answer supplied): Which delivery is most frequent in the reported history, and what denominator did you use? What does the uncertainty interval describe? What information is unavailable? Which statement would you use in preparation, and what prevents stronger reliance on it?

Rate clarity and usefulness separately on 1–5 (1=very poor, 3=mixed, 5=very good), with a reason and any missing information. Do not interpret these internal-reader ordinal ratings as a validated scale or independent coach endorsement. Preserve initial responses; after independent completion store discussion/resolutions separately. Report participation and missing/NA items explicitly. No judgments have been entered.
