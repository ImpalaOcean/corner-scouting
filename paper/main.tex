This working skeleton presents an event-based corner scouting pipeline, temporal
league prediction, retrospective spatial profiles and a cutoff-safe dossier example.
Saved evidence is distinguished from supplied execution reports. Additional dossiers,
reader evaluation and explicit scope decisions remain unfinished. This is not a final submission.

\section{Questions and corpus}
We investigate team-history delivery and shot prediction, static setup coverage and
historical scouting reports using pinned StatsBomb events~\cite{statsbomb} within P6-a~\cite{program2026}.
The corpus comprises 16,565 corners in 1,632 matches; Ligue 1 includes 377 listed matches.
This does not establish the proposed tens-of-thousands scale.
\section{Methods and audit}

\section{Prediction and robustness}
Chronological competition-season splits are 60/20/20, with eligibility fixed at
10 training matches and 30 training corners. Validation selects smoothing; test data
do not select methods. Match-bootstrap intervals use 1,000 draws and seed 5118.
The original experiment uses code 6a64fbdde56131077c66d245b2cb59cad60135a0.
Shrinkage improves delivery prediction in this corpus; the primary shot result
does not clearly improve on pooling. The unsmoothed delivery baseline floors
probabilities at $10^{-6}$ and renormalizes, so its contrast is floor-dependent.
Alternative windows are robustness analyses on reused matches, not replications.

\section{Retrospective spatial profiles}

These full-tournament fits are retrospective and cannot enter a prematch view.
